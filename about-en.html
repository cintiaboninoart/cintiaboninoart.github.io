Cintia Bonino

Editorial illustrator and animator based in Barcelona

ES | EN

I am an illustrator and visual artist based in Barcelona, specialised in editorial illustration, cultural posters, visual campaigns and 2D animation. I create narrative and conceptual imagery for press, cultural institutions, music and audiovisual projects.

I work across editorial, cultural and commercial projects, developing narrative and conceptual illustration with a poetic and expressive approach.

My work explores human relationships, identity and contemporary culture, combining a textured painterly aesthetic with intense colour and atmosphere. My visual language is influenced by 1990s–2000s visual culture, music and pop culture.

I have developed illustration and animation projects for media, cultural institutions, music and visual campaigns, integrating image and movement within a contemporary authorial approach. I work in Spanish and English and collaborate with local and international clients and projects.
