Cintia Bonino

Editorial illustrator and independent 2D animator based in Barcelona

Pop, irony and visual provocation.

ES | EN

I am an editorial illustrator and independent 2D animator based in Barcelona. I create narrative and conceptual images for editorial, cultural and music projects, working across illustration and animation. My approach combines pop culture, irony and visual provocation to build work with a distinctive graphic identity.

I work on editorial, cultural and commercial projects, creating expressive narrative and conceptual illustrations. I want images to do more than decorate: to offer a point of view, build an atmosphere or introduce an unexpected reading.

My work explores human relationships, identity and contemporary culture. I combine a textured, painterly aesthetic with colour, intense atmospheres and references to 1990s and 2000s visual culture and music. Irony and provocation are part of how I look at the world and build images.

I create illustration and animation for publications, cultural organisations, music projects and visual campaigns, bringing image and movement together through a distinctive, personal approach. I work in Spanish and English with local and international projects. For musicians, I create animated music videos in which illustration and movement offer a distinctive interpretation of the music.
