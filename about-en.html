Cintia Bonino

Editorial illustrator and independent 2D animator based in Barcelona

ES | EN

I like images that refuse to sit still or behave. I draw to look at reality sideways: its contradictions, oddities and all the things we would rather not see (but do anyway).

I work with illustration and animation for editorial, cultural, music and commercial projects. Sometimes an image explains things; sometimes it makes them even stranger. I’m interested in both. I’m after images with an idea behind them, their own atmosphere and, if possible, a little short circuit.

I’m drawn to human relationships, identity, pop culture and its contradictions. Also humour, irony and the everyday drama that feels written by someone with far too much free time. My visual world mixes texture, colour, 90s and 2000s references, music, pop culture and a variable dose of absurdity. Solemnity is not guaranteed. Irony and provocation are not decoration. They’re part of the machinery. There’s a millennial point of view in here: sceptical, curious, a fair amount of dark humour, little patience for perfect narratives and a soft spot for things that are slightly broken.

I make editorial illustration, images for cultural projects and campaigns, and hand-drawn frame-by-frame 2D animation. I also work with musicians, turning songs into images, characters and small visual universes. I work in Spanish and English, based in Barcelona and open to wherever a good idea might lead. For musicians, I draw and animate music videos, live visuals and release pieces. It’s not about putting pretty pictures on top of a song; it’s about finding its own visual delirium.
