What can I bring to your music video?

Illustration and animation conceived as one piece. The visuals are not an add-on to the music: they build atmosphere, tone and narrative. I am especially interested in projects that leave room for a personal reading, a strong aesthetic and intentional visual decisions.

  • 2D animated music videos.
  • Illustration and visual storytelling inspired by a song.
  • Development of a distinctive graphic language for each project.
  • Artist-led animation for musicians and cultural projects.

How we can work together

  1. Tell me about the idea. Tell me about the song and the music project, share your references, and explain what you want to communicate.
  2. Develop the visual approach. Together, we develop a visual direction that responds to the music rather than illustrating the lyrics literally.
  3. Bring it to life. Illustration, movement and rhythm come together in a coherent visual piece.

If you are looking for a music video with a strong visual identity and a clear artistic point of view, tell me what you have in mind.
