2D animation · Music · Visual direction

Animated music videos with a distinct identity.

I create author-led 2D animated music videos for musicians looking for more than moving images set to a song: a visual interpretation with character, illustration and a recognisable graphic universe.

Based in Barcelona, I develop each piece from an authorial perspective, connecting music, image and movement. The aim is not to apply a formula, but to find a visual language that fits each musical project.

What can I bring to your music video?

Illustration and animation conceived as one piece. The image is not an extra added to the music: it builds atmosphere, tone and narrative. I am especially interested in projects that leave room for a personal reading, a strong aesthetic and intentional visual decisions.

  • 2D animated music videos.
  • Illustration and visual storytelling inspired by a song.
  • Development of a distinctive graphic language for each project.
  • Author-led animation for musicians and cultural projects.

How we can get started

  1. Tell me about the idea. The song, the music project, your references and what you want to communicate.
  2. Find the visual approach. We explore a visual direction that responds to the music rather than simply illustrating lyrics literally.
  3. Develop the piece. Illustration, movement and rhythm come together in a coherent visual proposal.

If you are looking for a music video with a strong visual personality and an authorial point of view, tell me what you have in mind.
