Illustration · Ideas · Visual culture

Editorial illustration with a point of view.

I create narrative and conceptual illustrations for publications, press and cultural projects. I am interested in turning an idea into an image with character: expressive, suggestive and open to more than one reading.

My visual language combines texture, colour and references to pop culture, with a perspective that can be ironic, poetic or provocative depending on the project. Each image starts with an idea and looks for a visual form that expands it rather than simply repeating it.
